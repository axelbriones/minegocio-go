# MI NEGOCIO GO - BRANDING GUIDELINES

## 1. Brand Essence
**"La app debe funcionar como una libreta digital inteligente, no como un ERP."**

MI NEGOCIO GO is not a complex management software; it's the natural evolution of the business owner's notebook. It feels familiar, fast, and completely reliable even without an internet connection. The AI (MIGO) is invisible—it acts as a silent assistant that anticipates needs without ever getting in the way.

## 2. Visual Identity & Colors

The color palette is designed to evoke trust, speed, and approachability (resembling everyday apps like WhatsApp rather than corporate software).

*   **Primary Brand Color:** `Migo Green (#00A859)`
    *   *Usage:* Main CTAs, active states, and primary positive actions (Sales, Confirmations).
*   **Secondary Accent:** `Digital Blue (#2563EB)`
    *   *Usage:* Secondary actions, informative badges, sync status, and subtle MIGO AI highlights.
*   **Background / Base:** `Off-White (#F8FAFC)`
    *   *Usage:* App background, ensuring high contrast and readability.
*   **Surface / Cards:** `Pure White (#FFFFFF)`
    *   *Usage:* Product cards, modals, and input fields.
*   **Text (Primary):** `Slate Dark (#0F172A)`
    *   *Usage:* Headers, primary product names, main numerical values.
*   **Text (Secondary):** `Slate Muted (#64748B)`
    *   *Usage:* Subtitles, timestamps, minor details, placeholder text.
*   **Destructive / Alert:** `Alert Red (#EF4444)`
    *   *Usage:* Deletions, low stock warnings, offline error states.

## 3. Typography

**Primary Font Family: `Inter` (or system defaults like `Roboto` / `San Francisco`)**
*   **Headers (H1/H2):** Inter Bold, tight letter-spacing. Big, clear, and scannable.
*   **Body Text:** Inter Regular. High legibility for rapid reading.
*   **Numbers & Prices:** Inter SemiBold. Numbers are the most critical data points; they must be distinct and immediately recognizable.

## 4. Logo Structure

*   **Iconography:** The logo combines the universally recognized silhouette of a physical notebook with a subtle, stylized "spark" or "flash" (representing the invisible MIGO AI and speed).
*   **Wordmark:** "MI NEGOCIO" in heavy, confident sans-serif typography, with "GO" highlighted in the primary Migo Green, signifying action and forward momentum.
*   **Clear Space:** Must always maintain a generous padding to ensure readability in small app icon formats.

## 5. UI/UX Principles (The "No-ERP" Rule)

1.  **Chunky & Tap-Friendly:** Buttons must be large (minimum 48x48dp tap targets). No tiny icons.
2.  **High Contrast:** Essential for outdoor use or in brightly lit stores/warehouses.
3.  **One Action Per Screen:** Avoid visual clutter. If a screen is for selling, the only prominent element is the sale.
4.  **Invisible AI:** MIGO features should not look like complex "AI generation" prompts. They should look like natural suggested actions or auto-filled fields.

## 6. Tone of Voice

*   **Direct & Simple:** Say "Venta guardada" instead of "Transacción procesada exitosamente en la base de datos".
*   **Encouraging:** "Tienes 5 productos con bajo stock", not "Error de inventario: Stock crítico detectado".
*   **Conversational:** "MIGO: ¿Quieres guardar 20 Coca Colas 3L?"
